# Texts: vietnamese

The exact Vietnamese text of every English text a visitor reads on `/`, its 404 page and its search tags, approved with the prototype ("Prototype ổn", 2026-11-16; states `#vi-*` of `docs/design/prototype.html`). Voice "mình – bạn" (Q2). `*word*` marks the accent (italic) part, as on the English page. Words not listed (Ở, Hòa Xuân, Đà Nẵng, the chapter words ở nhà, ở gần, ở lâu, ở lại?, WhatsApp, Zalo, Pexels, numbers) stay as they are.
