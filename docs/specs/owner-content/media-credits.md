# Illustrative photos and the Hòa Xuân loop (epic 2)

Stock media that stands in for the house and the area until the owner's own photos exist (phase 2). All from Pexels, Pexels License (free for commercial use, no attribution required; https://www.pexels.com/license/). On the site each carries the chip "Illustrative photo" or "Illustrative video" and its credit.

They are added by hand before T3 starts (binary files cannot go through an issue), from the project files `epic-2-owner-content/media/`:

- photos: JPEG, longest side 1600px, quality 78, in `src/static/media/photos/<key>.jpg`;
- the loop: 10 s, muted, 1280x720, mp4 (H.264) and WebM, plus a JPEG poster from its first frame, in `src/static/media/area/area.{mp4,webm,jpg}`.

"Pexels" as the credit means the photographer is not named on the source page we recorded; the credit then reads "Photo: Pexels".
