# Intent: site shell and look (epic 1)

## Problem

The site today is one line of text on a blank page. A visitor who opens the link shared in an expat group sees nothing of the house, and gets no feeling of what living there is like. The owner wants the site to feel unique and different, not like another rental listing, while staying light, fast and found by search engines later.

## Goal

Every page of the site shares one recognisable look, "Living with Nature" as a quiet, editorial experience: large refined type, lots of air, places ready for photos and video, and colours that follow the real time of day in Hòa Xuân (dawn, day, dusk, night). The five pages exist with their addresses, the "page not found" page matches the look, and the search-engine groundwork is in place while the site stays out of search results.

## Users

- **Visitor:** a foreigner, a couple or a remote worker looking for a whole house to rent by the month in Đà Nẵng. Reads English. Often on a phone.
- **Owner (Bún):** shares the link and later fills the pages with photos and texts (epics 2 and 3).

## Out of scope

- The content of the five pages (hero text, tiles, gallery, FAQ answers, contact buttons): epic 3.
- Photos, videos and admin editing: epics 2 and 3. Epic 1 only shows the empty state of each photo or video place.
- The interactive floor plan on The house page: epic 3.
- Counting visits and clicks: epic 4.
- Vietnamese pages (/vi/): phase 3.
- Turning indexing on: phase 2.
