# Intent: the site as one page (epic 3)

## Problem

After epics 1 and 2 the site has its look, its settings and its photos, but nothing to read: the home page is the hero alone, and the four inner pages are headings only. A visitor from an expat group cannot learn what the house is, where it is, what it costs or how to reach the owner, so nobody gets in touch.

## Goal

One page tells the whole story in a single scroll: the hero, then five chapters (ở nhà the house, ở gần Hòa Xuân, ở lâu the rent, FAQ, ở lại? contact). The menu jumps to each chapter. Price and Call, WhatsApp and Zalo come from the owner's settings, the photos from the illustrative list. On a phone, the three contact buttons stay at hand at the bottom of the screen. The old addresses `/house/`, `/hoa-xuan/`, `/faq/` and `/contact/` lead to their chapter.

## Users

- **Visitor:** a foreigner, couple or remote worker looking for a whole house by the month in Đà Nẵng; reads English, mostly on a phone.
- **Owner (Bún):** changes the price and numbers in `/owner/`; the page follows.

## Out of scope

- Counting views and clicks: epic 4.
- Real photos, indexing, structured data: phase 2.
- Vietnamese: phase 3.
- The architect's floor plan: the plan stays a labelled schematic until the owner sends it.
