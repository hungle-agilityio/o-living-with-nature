# Intent: contact-one-place

## Problem

On a phone the home page shows the same three buttons (Call, WhatsApp, Zalo) three times: under the rent, under the FAQ and in the contact chapter. The phone bar that should carry them hides whenever one of those sets is on screen, so a visitor scrolling the page meets the same block again and again. In the contact chapter the form's "Send message" button looks exactly like the Call button above it, so the form does not read as a place to write.

## Goal

The buttons live in one place: in the contact chapter on a computer, and in a bar pinned to the bottom of a phone from chapter 02 (Hòa Xuân) down to the footer. The enquiry form sits on its own light panel and "Send message →" is the one filled button of the contact chapter.

## Users

Visitors looking for a long stay in Hòa Xuân, mostly on phones; the owner (Bún), who wants more calls and messages.

## Out of scope

- The journal note page keeps its own contact block.
- No new colour beyond the brand palette (no bright accent); no change to the counter's data or the owner pages.
- No change to the texts of the buttons or of the form, except the arrow after "Send message" / "Gửi lời nhắn".
