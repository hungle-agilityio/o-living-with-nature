# Intent: match the build to the design

## Problem

The one page on `main` (epics 1 to 3) does not look like the approved design. With JavaScript on, every chapter loses its container and side padding, so text and photos touch the edges of the screen on desktop and on phones. A template comment shows as raw text between Rent and FAQ. Some headings, the price line and the travel list differ from the design card. The page has none of the motion of the design E tour video, and the "Ở" hero only moves while the visitor scrolls through three screens.

## Goal

The page follows the approved prototype of design E "Through the Ở" (https://claude.ai/artifact/KGHzvGFPmKt8jEd95cxnEG, approved by the owner on 2026-11-12), built from the design E tour video, on desktop and phones. It replaces the layout of the epic 3 design card; the texts and the chapter order of epic 3 stay. The "Ở" plays by itself on load, like a short film, and ends on the full-screen video with the title. The page opens and reveals its chapters with the calm motion of the design E tour video, in the spirit of the reference site loiseau.framer.website, without copying its look or assets.

## Users

Visitors to the site (foreigners looking for a long stay), on desktop and phones.

## Out of scope

- New content, texts, photos or chapters.
- The floor plan's look (the spec of epic 3, AC16, stays).
- Vietnamese pages.
- Any change to the time-of-day themes, the contact counter (epic 4) or hosting.
