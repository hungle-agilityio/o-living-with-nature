# Intent: house-plan-2d

## Problem

"Walk through the house" on the home page is a grid of ten labelled boxes. It does not show the shape of the house, so a visitor cannot picture how the rooms sit together: the light well, the veranda, the bedrooms at the back. Only five boxes open a photo, and no room says anything about itself.

## Goal

The section becomes a hand-drawn 2D drawing of the house, in the style of the owner's watercolour sketch: a small section above a plan, read from the owner's cardboard model of the real house. Tapping a room shows its photo (or an empty block until there is one) and a sentence or two about it. Three photos of the cardboard model sit under the drawing.

## Users

Visitors of `/` and `/vi/` (people thinking of renting the whole house for a month or more), on phones and on desktops.

## Out of scope

- Real photos of the rooms (Phase 2, paused until the house is built): the room photos stay the licensed stock photos labelled "Illustrative photo".
- The architect's plan, measurements or room sizes.
- Any change to the owner pages, the other chapters or the database.
