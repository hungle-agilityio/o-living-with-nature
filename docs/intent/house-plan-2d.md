# Intent: house-plan-2d

## Problem

"Walk through the house" on the home page is a grid of ten labelled boxes. It does not show the shape of the house, so a visitor cannot picture how the rooms sit together: the garden and the kitchen side by side at the gate, the bathroom and laundry on either side of the hall, the bedrooms at the back. Only five boxes open a photo, and no room says anything about itself.

## Goal

The section becomes a hand-drawn 2D drawing of the house, in the style of the owner's watercolour sketch: a small section above a plan of the 6 × 18 m plot, laid out as the owner described the real plan (2026-11-19: garden 3.5 m and kitchen 2.5 m wide at the gate, then bathroom, hall and laundry, then two bedrooms and a triangle of back garden). Tapping a room shows its photo (or an empty block until there is one) and a sentence or two about it. The list "The layout" above it goes: the drawing names the rooms. The facts row above is drawn in the same sketch style, and its third fact becomes "1 real kitchen" (2 bedrooms, 2 gardens, 1 real kitchen, 1 household), since the real plan has no light well.

## Users

Visitors of `/` and `/vi/` (people thinking of renting the whole house for a month or more), on phones and on desktops.

## Out of scope

- Real photos of the rooms (Phase 2, paused until the house is built): the room photos stay the licensed stock photos labelled "Illustrative photo".
- The architect's plan, measurements or room sizes beyond the ones the owner gave (6 × 18 m plot, 3.5 m and 2.5 m wide, 9 m deep).
- The other places on the page that still name a light well or a veranda (the photo collage of chapter 01): a later change.
- Any change to the owner pages, the other chapters or the database.
