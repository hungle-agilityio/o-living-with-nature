# Domain

## Terms

- **The house:** one garden house for rent in Hòa Xuân (Đà Nẵng), two bedrooms. It is not built yet.
- **Rental:** the whole house, rented monthly, long term. Not by the room, not by the night.
- **Visitor:** someone reading the site, in Vietnamese or English.
- **Owner:** the person who rents out the house and answers contacts.

## Business rules

- **Pages of phase 1 (five):** Home, House, Hòa Xuân area, FAQ, Contact. No blog.
- **Languages:** every page exists in Vietnamese and English with the same content.
- **Contact:** phone, WhatsApp, Zalo and email. The site shows them; it does not send messages for the visitor.
- **Content:** written in the templates (no CMS). The base URL of the site is set in one place (`SITE_BASE_URL`).
- **Purpose:** early search-engine visibility before the house is built, so pages must be crawlable and say clearly that the house is not finished yet.
- Never invent facts about the house (price, size, dates, photos, amenities): they come from the owner, in an issue.
